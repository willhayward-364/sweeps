Rules

Version v0.14  •  Last deployed 2026-08-03 20:14:10 UTC  •  API last fetched 2026-08-03 20:14:09 UTC

Points are awarded to each player based on the outcomes of the matches involving their selected teams.

R1
Win bonus (3 points)
A selected team that wins a match earns 3 points for the player.
R2
Draw bonus (1 point)
A selected team that draws a match earns 1 point for the player.
R3
Clean sheet bonus (1 point)
If a selected team keeps a clean sheet, the player earns 1 extra point.
R4
Goal feast bonus (1 point)
If a selected team scores 3 or more goals, the player earns 1 extra point.
R5
Giant-killer bonus (3 points)
If a selected team from Pot 3 or Pot 4 beats a Pot 1 team, the player earns 3 extra points.
